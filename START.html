Unit 3 review

Cell structure

  • Cell membrane: controls what enters and leaves the cell (selectively permeable).
  • Nucleus: contains DNA; controls cell activities.
  • Mitochondria: site of aerobic respiration, releases energy.
  • Ribosomes: where proteins are made.
  • Plant cells also have a cell wall (cellulose), chloroplasts, and a permanent vacuole.

Diffusion and osmosis

  • Diffusion: net movement of particles from high to low concentration.
  • Osmosis: diffusion of water across a partially permeable membrane.
  • Rate increases with temperature, concentration gradient, and surface area.
